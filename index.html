How to Test (quick guide)

Keep conditions consistent: same surface, same warm-up, same technique. Record your best attempt.

20m Sprint
  • Flat surface (track best)
  • Standing start
  • 2–3 attempts, full rest
Add video: paste YouTube embed in code later.
Vertical Jump
  • Wall + chalk or device
  • Two-foot takeoff
  • Record best of 2–3
Broad Jump
  • Two-foot takeoff
  • Measure to back of heel
  • Stick landing
Push-ups
  • Full lockout
  • Consistent depth
  • Stop when form breaks
1km Run
  • Track or flat route
  • Continuous effort
  • Enter as mm.ss (3.56)
